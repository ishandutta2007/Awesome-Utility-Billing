# Awesome-Utility-Billing

## Top Utility Billing Platforms Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Utility Customer Information Systems (CIS), Meter-to-Cash Billing, Payments, Rate Management & Municipal/Utility Operations*  

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Utility Billing**. These systems help water, electric, gas, and multi-utility providers manage customer accounts, meter data, complex rate structures, billing cycles, payments, collections, and customer service.



**Examples** include OpenGov Utility Billing, Tyler Utility Billing, InvoiceCloud, Harris Utilities, Springbrook, Cayenta, CentralSquare Utility Billing, Muni-Link, Edmunds GovTech, UBmax, Oracle Utilities CCS, Harris NorthStar, Advanced Utility Systems, Utilibill, Gentrack, Kraken, Powerley, MuniBilling, and UBMax (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for utility billing, meter-to-cash workflows, ERP-based billing modules, and related open tools — ideal for municipalities, co-ops, smaller utilities, and developers seeking customizable or self-hosted alternatives to proprietary CIS platforms.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[ERPNext Utility Billing & Property Management](https://github.com/navariltd/utility-billing)**  

  Open-source ERPNext app for utility billing, meter readings, tariffs, mass invoicing, and property/tenant management suitable for municipal and multi-service providers.



- **[BillRun](https://github.com/BillRun/system)**  

  Open-source enterprise billing and rating system originally designed for telecom and usage-based services; adaptable for complex utility rating and invoicing scenarios.



- **[FOSSBilling](https://github.com/FOSSBilling/FOSSBilling)**  

  Fully open-source billing and client management platform that can be extended for recurring utility-style invoicing and customer portals.



- **[Water Billing System Prototypes](https://github.com/mugash/water-sys)**  

  Community open-source projects focused on water utility billing, meter management, and basic customer invoicing workflows.



- **[ERPNext / Frappe Accounting & Billing Core](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Full open-source ERP foundation with strong accounting, invoicing, payment entry, and multi-company capabilities that serve as a base for custom utility billing solutions.



- **[Odoo Accounting & Utility Extensions](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Community modules and custom developments on Odoo for recurring billing, meter-based invoicing, and utility customer management.



- **[Java / PHP Water Billing Systems](https://github.com/jmagwili/Water-Billing-System)**  

  Open-source desktop and web prototypes for water services billing, including meter reading entry, rate management, and bill generation.



- **[General Meter-to-Cash Scripts & Tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Community repositories containing meter data processing, rate engine examples, and invoice generation scripts for utilities.



- **[Open Banking & Payment Integration Libraries](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source tools for payment gateway integration, ACH, and electronic bill presentment that complement utility billing systems.



- **[Rate Engine & Tariff Calculation Projects](https://github.com/)**  

  Academic and community open-source implementations of complex utility rate structures and billing determinants.



- **[Customer Portal & Self-Service Frameworks](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source portals that can be paired with billing backends for customer account viewing, payments, and service requests.



- **[Meter Data Management Helpers](https://github.com/)**  

  Tools and libraries for handling AMI/AMR meter data imports that feed into billing systems.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **ERP foundations**: ERPNext and Odoo Community as the most practical bases for building custom utility CIS and billing functionality.

- **Billing engines**: BillRun and FOSSBilling for rating, invoicing, and recurring charge management.

- **Specialized prototypes**: Multiple water, electric, and municipal billing projects on GitHub that demonstrate meter-to-cash flows.

- **Supporting components**: Open-source payment processors, PDF invoice generators, and notification systems.

- Many regional and academic **utility billing** and **CIS** experiments continue to appear on GitHub.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **ERPNext (with Utility Billing app) or Odoo** for core customer, meter, and invoicing logic, open payment and banking connectors, rate calculation modules, and a customer self-service portal to create a practical self-hosted utility billing platform.
